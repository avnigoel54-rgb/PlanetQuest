Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. While some changes are natural, modern warming is driven mainly by human activities, especially burning fossil fuels and deforestation.

Why it matters

  • Melting ice and rising sea levels threaten coastal communities.
  • Extreme weather events like storms, heatwaves, and droughts are increasing.
  • Shifts in habitats and food supply affect biodiversity and livelihoods.

What you can do

  1. Reduce energy use: switch off unused lights, choose efficient appliances.
  2. Travel smart: walk, cycle, carpool, or use public transport where possible.
  3. Support renewable energy and sustainable policies in your community.

Did you know?

Human activities have caused the Earth's average temperature to rise by about 1.1°C since the late 19th century.
